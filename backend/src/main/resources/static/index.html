SELECTED EXECUTION MODE

Automatic trading

NSE cash · 5 min

Two dedicated data workers cover NIFTY 50 and other maintained NIFTY 500 stocks. News supplies up to three primary candidates from today, or the previous seven days. When price fallback is enabled and fresh news collection succeeds, qualifying news stocks take priority; otherwise affordable candle candidates from both groups can qualify. Other-stock prices are screened in batches and candles are loaded for at most 12 other stocks per cycle; this is not a complete 500-stock candle scan every five seconds. When a successful fresh news check has no directional candidate, the configured fallback checks maintained stocks below INR 500 using candles. Configured price-band and risk limits apply to future entries. Real orders also require broker margin approval. Trading AI confirms one BUY / LONG or SELL / SHORT candidate with candles and risk rules. All available completed shortlist candles are shared with AI about every five seconds. A qualifying response goes through final quote and risk checks before one entry in the selected mode. Keep one active position until target, stop or the 14:59 IST exit deadline.

Share-price limits by cash capital · paper and real

Uses effective cash capital before leverage. Ranges apply in order. Primary news and candle analysis stays visible when a stock is too expensive; it cannot authorize entry. Stop and target price bands

Bands are consecutive, with exclusive upper limits; the last band covers all higher prices. These editable price-band defaults originated in paper testing. Real quantities require Groww MIS margin, funds and independent risk approval. The real exposure ceiling starts at 1× until you save a higher cap.

Administrator hard limits

A separate administrator token is required to save hard limits or reset the kill switch.

Daily loss limit = cash allocation × selected-mode leverage × configured daily loss percentage. Example: ₹1,800 × 5 × 2% = ₹180. The cash allocation is the lower of effective capital and the position cash cap. This uses the configured exposure ceiling; actual real buying power still requires Groww margin approval. Per-trade risk also uses configured intraday exposure: ₹1,800 × 5 × 0.5% = ₹45. Dashboard and administrator percentage limits both apply; the stricter value wins. Realized and open P&L after estimated costs count toward this limit. When reached, new entries stop for the day and managed positions request an exit. Real exits require broker-confirmed fills.
